title: 'unplugged'
pubDate: '2026-02-19'
description: 'my brain is overstimulated and my bucket list is being cannibalized by infinite feeds.'
---

i have never been a fan of social media. i’ve had awesome connections here, long night conversations and what have you (i think i’ve experienced it all). and yet, every time i land back in the spot i once left, i am tired. i want to be stripped of these attention-draining apps, locking the backdoor and throwing the key into a deep, metaphoric ocean where finding it wouldn't be possible anymore.

i have multiple items on my bucket list and my socials keep **cannibalizing** my progress. it’s not an argument about discipline, it’s about my environment. i prune like a lawn mower, everything present has my attention, so i’m clearing the field. if you’re wondering about friends, family, or school, i can reach them via iMessage and WhatsApp. other socials drain my energy rather than provide value, i even have all statuses on my whatsapp muted and barely checked.

growing up, i’ve learnt one thing: life is about choices and consequences. after much consideration, and keeping in mind that i won't be using my socials as a creator, **at least for now**, i’ve made my choice. i enjoy creating short films, if i do, i’ll upload them to my youtube. i take photos for fun, so i created a private <u>[gallery to showcase the images i fancy.](https://gallery.justsomto.dev)</u>

but for everything else, i am fine making use of just <u>[X](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://x.com/snthegr8)</u> and <u>[GitHub.](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/snthegr8)</u>

i might take down my linkedin profile, or at least delete the app and check it only at an **infrequent, almost accidental interval**. my focus will be solely on X, GitHub (to read through OSS), and Feedly, where i have all my favorite reads like *browndroppings* and other cool blogs. i’ll be deleting my instagram, snapchat, and tiktok. i’m uninstalling facebook, though i’ll leave my account as-is since i don't have a use case for it anymore. i don't wish to partake in the constant information overload.

my brain is overstimulated. this is simply a way to recalibrate and build myself into the version i want to be.
